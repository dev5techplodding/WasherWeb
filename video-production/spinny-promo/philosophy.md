# Production Philosophy: Spinny — "Buy Back Your Weekend"

## Visual Identity
- **Art Style**: Premium minimalism, photorealistic cinematic quality — editorial/magazine-grade lifestyle photography, never stock-photo cliché. Subtle 3D/glassmorphism accents (frosted glass cards, soft backdrop blur) reserved for app-UI moments only.
- **Color Palette**: Spinny Orange (#F28C28) as the singular accent/highlight color — used sparingly and deliberately (app UI, logo, one hero prop) against warm-neutral surroundings. Deep Navy (#122840) and Blue (#1B3A5C) ground darker/interior shots. Warm Gray (#F8F9FA) and Peach (#FFB993) carry soft daytime/interior scenes. Golden-hour warmth throughout — never cold or clinical.
- **Lighting**: Warm, natural, golden-hour tones; soft shadows; no harsh fluorescents. Interiors feel like a lived-in, aspirational urban Canadian home, not a studio.
- **Composition**: Clean whitespace equivalents in-frame (uncluttered backgrounds), intentional negative space for later text overlay, rule-of-thirds framing on hero subjects (person, driver, item).

## Motion Language
- **Movement Quality**: Smooth, fluid, confident — never frantic. Motion should feel like time being given back, not chaos being resolved.
- **Pacing**: Apple-keynote-inspired — deliberate, unhurried holds on key beats, smooth accelerations at transitions. No fast-cut chaos editing.
- **Camera Style**: Cinematic dolly/tracking/push moves; occasional slow handheld warmth for lifestyle intimacy; no shaky or whip-pan camera work.

## Subject Consistency
- **Characters**: A busy urban professional (25–45, diverse/multicultural casting reflecting Canada) recurs across scenes as the throughline customer. Wardrobe: modern, casual-professional, condo/townhouse setting.
- **Environment**: Modern Canadian metro apartment/condo interior (Toronto/Vancouver-coded, no specific landmarks needed) + doorstep/hallway exterior for pickup-delivery beats.
- **Props/Elements**: Folded laundry, a pair of clean sneakers, a fresh duvet, a leather bag, the Spinny app on a modern phone, a branded driver tote/bag with the orange-and-blue "S" mark.

## Constraints
- **Avoid**: Dirty/messy "before" imagery, competitor brand colors (no gold/yellow that reads as Rinse), generic laundromat stock-photo look, cold/corporate tone, cluttered frames, laundry-only framing (must read as whole-wardrobe-and-home).
- **Maintain**: Spinny Orange as the only saturated accent color; warm/friendly tone; the "time bought back" emotional payoff (relief, ease, a reclaimed weekend) as the throughline of every scene; item variety (not laundry alone) across the montage.
