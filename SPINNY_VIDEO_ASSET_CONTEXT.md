    ## What Spinny (this project) actually is

    - **Category:** Time-buyback concierge marketplace for wardrobe & home care — pickup, specialist cleaning/care, delivery.
    - **NOT** a car company, ride service, or vehicle marketplace of any kind.
    - **NOT** just a laundry app — it also covers footwear/leather, home textiles (duvets, curtains, rugs), and tailoring/alterations.
    - **Market:** Canada — major metros (Toronto, Vancouver, Montreal, Calgary, Ottawa, Edmonton).
    - **Model:** Pay-per-use by default; optional cancel-anytime "Spinny Membership" for recurring pickups. Membership is never required.
    - **Three-sided marketplace:** Customers ↔ local specialist Vendors (laundromats, dry cleaners, cobblers/leather-care, textile cleaners) ↔ independent Drivers (pickup/delivery logistics).

    ## Core message for any video/asset

    **One-liner:** *Not a laundry company. The concierge service that buys back your time — for your whole wardrobe and home.*
