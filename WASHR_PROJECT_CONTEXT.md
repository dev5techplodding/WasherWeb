# Spinny — Project Context & Brand Brief

## What is Spinny?

**Spinny** is a Canada-based **time-buyback service** for your wardrobe and home. It started as an on-demand laundry and dry cleaning platform — it has since grown into a full concierge for everything a household maintains: garments, footwear and leather goods, home textiles, and special-care items. Spinny connects customers with vetted specialist vendors (laundry, footwear/leather, home-textile, tailoring) and independent drivers, creating a seamless pickup → specialist care → deliver experience through a single app.

> **One-liner:** *Not a laundry company. The concierge service that buys back your time — for your whole wardrobe and home, not just your laundry.*

| **Website** | spinny.ca |

### How It Works

1. **Schedule a Pickup** — customer selects a time slot and what needs care: laundry, a pair of sneakers, a duvet, whatever's on the list
2. **Driver Arrives** — a Spinny driver collects the item(s) from the customer's door
3. **Specialist Care** — each item goes to the vendor matched to its material: a laundry partner, a footwear/leather specialist, or a home-textile facility — never a one-size-fits-all cycle
4. **Delivered Back, Time Bought Back** — items return closet-ready or drawer-ready, and the hours it used to take are the customer's again

## The Three-Sided Marketplace

Spinny operates as a **three-sided platform** connecting:

### 1. Customers
- Busy professionals, families, students, and anyone who values their time
- People who want their whole wardrobe and home cared for without the hassle of five separate errands
- Urban Canadians in metro areas

### 2. Service Vendors (Partners)
- Local laundromats, dry cleaners, footwear & leather specialists, home-textile cleaners, and tailoring/alterations businesses
- Spinny helps them **get more orders** and **connect online** — expanding their customer base beyond walk-ins
- Vendors set their own pricing and service offerings
- Dashboard for order management, analytics, and customer reviews

### 3. Drivers
- Independent drivers who handle pickup and delivery logistics
- Flexible gig-economy work with optimized routing
- Spinny provides the driver service infrastructure to vendors who don't have their own delivery capability

## Unique Selling Points (USPs)

### 🕐 Time Bought Back, Not Just Clothes Cleaned
> **Spinny's core promise isn't "clean clothes." It's the 5+ hours a week those clothes (and shoes, and duvets, and rugs) were quietly costing.**
> Every feature is judged by one question: does this give the customer their time back?

### 🧥 One Service, Entire Living Space
Suits and sneakers. Duvets and diaper bags. Curtains and cashmere. Spinny is the single point of contact for every fabric, leather, and textile surface in a customer's home — no juggling five specialists for one household.

### 🚫 No Forced Subscriptions
> Pay-per-use remains the default — order once a year or five times a week, no lock-in, no minimum spend, no cancellation fees. **Spinny Membership** exists as an optional upgrade for households who want pickups fully automated, not a requirement to use the service.

### 🚗 Driver-Powered Logistics
Spinny provides a full driver network to local vendors, enabling even small neighborhood specialists to offer pickup & delivery without hiring their own fleet.

### 🏪 Empowering Local Specialists
Rather than building mega-facilities, Spinny partners with existing local businesses — laundromats, cobblers, leather-care studios, textile cleaners — giving them online visibility, order flow, and delivery infrastructure they couldn't afford alone.

### 💎 Investment-Grade Care
- Commercial-grade cleaning equipment, matched to material (fabric, leather, suede, wool, down)
- Eco-friendly, hypoallergenic detergents
- Individual item inspection — a $400 sneaker gets the same scrutiny as a formal shirt
- Professional folding, hanging, and packaging — closet-ready, not just "clean"
- Stain treatment, restoration, and special-care options (baby gear, pet bedding, wedding dress preservation)

### ⚡ Convenience-First UX
- Schedule pickups in under 30 seconds — for any category
- Real-time order tracking
- In-app communication with drivers
- Transparent pricing — no hidden fees
- Rating system for quality assurance

## Asset Generation Guidelines

When generating **videos, images, banners, social media content, or marketing assets** for Spinny, use this context:

### Do's ✅
- Use the **Spinny Orange (#F28C28)** as the primary accent color
- Feature **the full ecosystem** — folded laundry, restored sneakers, leather goods, folded rugs/curtains — not laundry alone
- Show the **app interface** on modern phones when relevant
- Emphasize **time-buyback messaging first** ("hours reclaimed," "your weekend back") — pay-per-use / optional-membership pricing is a supporting point, not the headline
- Include the **Spinny logo** (orange-and-blue swirl "S" mark + "Spinny" wordmark)
- Use **Canadian imagery** — maple elements subtly, Canadian cities
- Highlight the **three-sided marketplace** (customers, specialist vendors, drivers)
- Show **doorstep delivery** moments, across categories (not just a laundry bag)

### Don'ts ❌
- Don't use generic stock photos of laundry
- Don't frame Spinny as "just" a laundry or dry-cleaning app — the wardrobe/footwear/home scope should read clearly
- Don't imply membership is required — pay-per-use is still the default; membership is optional
- Don't use competitor brand colors (Rinse gold, etc.)
- Don't show dirty/messy items — always clean, aspirational, "after" imagery
- Don't use overly corporate or cold visual tones
- Don't forget the warmth — Spinny is friendly, not clinical

### Asset Formats Commonly Needed
- **Social Media:** Instagram (1080×1080, 1080×1920), Facebook (1200×630), Twitter/X (1600×900), LinkedIn (1200×627)
- **Web Banners:** Hero (1920×1080), CTA sections, feature cards
- **Video:** 16:9 (1920×1080) for web, 9:16 (1080×1920) for stories/reels
- **Print:** Business cards, flyers, vehicle wraps for driver fleet
- **App Store:** Screenshots (1290×2796 iPhone, 1080×1920 Android)
